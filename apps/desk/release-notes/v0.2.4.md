## Reelfold 0.2.4 · 千剪 0.2.4

Autopilot: say what you want, start the next one, and come back to finished clips.

### Highlights

- **Autopilot by default**: a request from Home becomes a project at once and runs to finished clips — no plan to
  confirm. Up to two run side by side, the rest wait in line. Every call the AI made is listed and can be changed.
  Prefer to check first? Switch to **Ask me first**.
- **Control room**: All projects shows what needs you, what is running (with each step and time left), what is ready
  and what is scheduled — open any clip, change a decision, schedule the ready ones.
- **Start from words alone**: no footage needed for explainers, series, scripts or slides; paste links or a public
  Notion page (or drop an exported Notion folder) and Reelfold reads them first.
- **Fewer steps in the editor**: deleting words in the transcript and AI edits apply at once, with Undo.
- **Record yourself, redesigned**: teleprompter right under the lens, one record button with a countdown, choose
  camera and mic, keep the best take of each line, then **Finish — make my video**.
- **Skin smoothing**: ask for 磨皮 / beauty and it is applied, face-tracked and natural.
- **No more files to write**: steps that used to ask for a config file are drafted for you and shown in plain words.
- Fixes: Home never gets stuck on a failed plan; the Inbox never shows raw config; the engine no longer hangs while a
  project renders.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- 默认自动驾驶：在首页说一句话就直接变成项目，自动剪完，不用先确认方案；最多同时跑两个，其余排队。AI 做的每个决定都能看到、能改。想先确认？切到「先问我」。
- 控制台：「全部项目」一眼看到需要你处理的、正在跑的（每一步和剩余时间）、已完成和已排期的；可打开任意片段、修改决定、一键排期。
- 没有素材也能开始：科普、系列、脚本、幻灯片都可以；粘贴链接或公开的 Notion 页面（或拖入导出的 Notion 文件夹），先读再规划。
- 编辑更少步骤：在文字稿里删字、让 AI 改，立即生效，可撤销。
- 重新设计的「自己录」：提词器就在镜头下方，一个录制按钮带倒计时，可选摄像头和麦克风，每句保留最好的一条，最后「完成 — 生成视频」。
- 磨皮美颜：说「磨个皮」即可，跟踪人脸，自然不假面。
- 不再需要手写配置文件：原来要你写文件的步骤，现在自动起草，用大白话给你确认。
- 修复：首页不会再卡在「没能出方案」；收件箱不再显示原始配置；项目渲染时引擎不再卡死。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。
