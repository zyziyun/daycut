## Reelfold 0.2.1 · 千剪 0.2.1

A small update on top of 0.2.0, the first public release: better talking-head defaults and post copy.

### Highlights

- **Talking heads default to the notes look**: clean caption cards with keywords you review before the render.
- **Post title and body from the final captions**: each clip gets a draft title and post text written from what
  you actually said after the edit, ready to paste or fill into the upload page.
- **Mac App Store edition prepared**: the sandboxed Lite build names no other download and links nowhere.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- 口播默认使用「笔记」风格：干净的字幕卡片，关键词渲染前由你确认。
- 根据最终字幕自动起草每条视频的标题和正文，可直接粘贴或填入上传页。
- 为 Mac App Store 版做好准备：沙盒版不再出现其它下载入口或外链。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。
