## Reelfold 0.2.5 · 千剪 0.2.5

One Studio for every video: watch them all being made, then finish each one on a single page.

### Highlights

- **Studio**: every video in one list — needs you / in progress / ready / scheduled — with its current step and time
  left. Pick one and everything is on one page: title, player, covers, caption looks, transcript, post copy and
  schedule, plus one AI bar. Title + copy + cover + schedule now takes 4 clicks.
- **Fewer places**: the menu is just Studio · Calendar · Settings; "＋ New video" (⌘N) starts anything.
- **Re-record a line (pickups)**: when you stop recording you land in the editor; select words and press Re-record
  or Add after — the new bit is matched in loudness and room tone.
- **Updates you can't miss**: "Update ready — Restart to update", with what's new.
- **Stay on top**: Dock badge, notifications that open the exact clip, ⌘K to any video.
- **Does what you asked**: Chinese captions when you ask for them, captions inside the frame, relevant hashtags,
  clips that end on a full sentence, the brand spelled right, faster plans for recordings.
- Fixes: screen sharing works (and says how to allow it), the transcript shows at once, edits reach the published
  video, the engine no longer stops on a stray signal.
- Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple (macOS, Apple silicon); Windows x64 is an unsigned preview.

### 更新（中文）

- Studio：所有视频在一个列表里——要你看 / 进行中 / 可以发 / 已排期，每条显示当前步骤和剩余时间；点开一条，标题、播放器、封面、字幕样式、逐字稿、文案和排期都在同一页，另有一个 AI 输入框。改标题+文案+封面+排期只要 4 次点击。
- 入口更少：菜单只剩 Studio · 日历 · 设置；「＋ 新视频」（⌘N）开始一切。
- 补录：录完直接进编辑页；选中文字按「重录」或「在后面补」，补录的音量和环境声自动匹配。
- 更新不再错过：「有新版本 — 重启更新」，附更新说明。
- 随时掌握：程序坞角标、通知直接打开对应片段、⌘K 跳到任意视频。
- 说到做到：要中文字幕就给中文字幕、字幕不出画、话题标签相关、片段在整句结束、品牌名拼对、录口播出方案更快。
- 修复：屏幕共享可用（未授权时告诉你怎么开）、逐字稿立即显示、修改会同步到发布的视频、引擎不会再被意外信号停掉。
- macOS（Apple 芯片）已签名并经 Apple 公证；Windows x64 为未签名预览版。
