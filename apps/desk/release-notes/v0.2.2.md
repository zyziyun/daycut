## Reelfold 0.2.2 · 千剪 0.2.2

Fixes from editing a real 12-minute talking-head review in the app, plus a richer promo template.

### Highlights

- **Promo template, less talking head**: screen recordings play full screen with you as a small tile bottom-right;
  animated cards (ranking, bar chart, stat, flow, checklist, ...) and finished clips play next to you; hooks open
  the video; browser bars are cropped out of screen recordings automatically.
- **Nothing you attach is dropped**: every attached recording and folder of clips reaches the project.
- **See what is happening**: the plan card shows real progress (transcribing x / y min, scanning, asking the AI),
  and the project page shows an agent's live status.
- **Clearer checkpoints and errors**: "write your part" steps show what to write and where; a failed render names
  the tool that failed (e.g. a broken Node) and how to fix it.
- **Your persona is used everywhere**: speeds, cleanup strength and covers follow your settings in the app too;
  covers no longer warp your face, and Chinese quotes no longer show as boxes.
- **Watermark**: your handle or logo on every export (Settings › Watermark).

### 更新（中文）

- 作品宣传模板：录屏全屏 + 右下角小头像；数据动画卡（排名、柱状图、对比数字、流程图、清单等）和成片在分屏里播放；开头可加 hooks；录屏自动裁掉浏览器栏。
- 附加的录屏和素材文件夹不再被丢掉。
- 出方案时显示真实进度（转写到第几分钟、扫描、调用 AI），项目页显示 agent 的实时状态。
- 「写你的部分」检查点说明要写什么、写在哪；渲染失败会说明是哪个工具出错、怎么修。
- App 也读取你的 persona（语速、清理强度、封面）；封面不再拉变形，中文引语不再显示成方框。
- 水印：在每条导出视频上加你的账号名或 logo（设置 › 水印）。
